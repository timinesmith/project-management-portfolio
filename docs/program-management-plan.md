# Sanitized Program Management Plan

**Status:** Illustrative demonstration based on verified public-sector outreach experience. Names, dates, and operational details are generalized.

## 1. Purpose

Coordinate a multi-location community-engagement program supporting a major transportation improvement initiative. The plan aligns agency stakeholders, consultants, field supervisors, outreach staff, and community partners.

## 2. Objectives

1. Deliver a predictable schedule of public-facing engagement activities.
2. Capture resident and rider feedback in a consistent format.
3. Escalate access, safety, and logistics issues within one business day.
4. Produce weekly summaries leaders can use for planning and public communication.

## 3. Governance

| Function | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Program sponsor | Final decisions, public commitments, funding approvals |
| Program manager | Integrated plan, schedule, risks, status reporting |
| Field supervisor | Daily deployment readiness, staff assignments, issue escalation |
| Stakeholder lead | Partner scheduling, briefing materials, follow-up log |
| Data/reporting lead | Activity counts, feedback themes, weekly dashboard |

## 4. Workstreams

- Deployment planning
- Stakeholder coordination
- Staff scheduling and training
- Materials and logistics
- Field reporting and quality review
- Issue and risk management

## 5. Operating cadence

- Daily field huddle during active deployment weeks
- Twice-weekly consultant coordination check-in
- Weekly stakeholder status report
- Monthly milestone and risk review

## 6. Acceptance criteria

A deployment is complete when staffing is confirmed, materials are checked, the activity log is submitted, public feedback is categorized, follow-up owners are assigned, and exceptions are entered in the issue log.
