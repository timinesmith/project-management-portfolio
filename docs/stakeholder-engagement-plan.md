# Stakeholder Engagement Plan

**Status:** Illustrative demonstration. This plan contains stakeholder categories only; it intentionally excludes names and contact information from real program files.

## Stakeholder groups

| Group | Interest | Engagement method | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Program sponsor | Scope, schedule, public commitments | Decision briefing and dashboard | Weekly |
| Municipal partners | Local coordination and public impact | Planning meeting and written summary | Biweekly |
| Community organizations | Access, outreach quality, feedback themes | Briefing and listening session | Monthly or event-based |
| Field staff | Assignments, safety, materials, escalation | Huddle and deployment packet | Each deployment |
| General public | Service information and opportunity to comment | Pop-up activity and survey | Scheduled events |

## Message discipline

- State what is changing, when it may change, and what remains undecided.
- Separate confirmed facts from planning assumptions.
- Record every public commitment in the follow-up log with an owner and due date.
- Route technical questions to the responsible subject-matter lead rather than improvising an answer.

## Follow-up standard

A stakeholder interaction is not closed until the question is answered, referred to a named owner, or explicitly logged as pending with a review date.
